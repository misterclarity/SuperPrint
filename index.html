Getting a good print

A few things worth knowing

Paper matters most

Plain copier paper is fine for pencils. If you like markers or any kind of wet media, 120 gsm or heavier cardstock stops the bleed-through.

Print at 100%

Turn off “fit to page” and set margins to none. Pages are drawn with a safe margin already, so they land inside any printer's printable area.

Grayscale saves ink

The artwork is pure black line on white. Printing in black-and-white or draft mode costs almost nothing and looks identical.

Everything happens on your device. There is no server, no upload and no account. Designs you save live in this browser's local storage, and a design's link contains everything needed to redraw it.

Questions

Good to know

What does “AI generated” mean here?

Each style is a generative drawing model: a set of rules plus a stream of seeded randomness that composes petals, facets, ribbons or contour lines into a finished page. Nothing is retrieved from a library of pre-made images — the artwork is computed on your device the moment you ask for it, which is why the supply is effectively endless and every page is genuinely new.

Can I use these commercially?

Yes. Pages you generate are yours to print, share, sell in a colouring book, or hand out at a workshop or care home. No attribution required, though it is always welcome.

How do I get the same design back later?

Every page has a seed like amber-thistle-408. Save the design to your collection, copy its link, or simply write the seed down and type it into the studio again — the same seed and settings always redraw the identical page.

Which format should I download?

Print directly from the browser for everyday use. Choose PNG at 300 DPI for photo printers and print shops, and SVG if you want to scale the artwork to poster size, edit it in Illustrator or Inkscape, or send it to a cutting machine.

Does it work offline?

Once the page has loaded, yes — the generators run entirely in the browser, so you can keep creating and printing without a connection.
